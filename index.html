A few things to know

Before you
get started.

The essentials for downloading, connecting and keeping your app up to date.

Where does the download button take me?

It opens the official v1.0.27 GitHub release. Under Assets, choose the Windows Setup installer to install Vidu Digit Trader.

Can I use a demo account?

Yes. The app supports Deriv demo accounts. Start with demo while you learn the strategies and understand their behaviour.

Will the app update automatically?

The installed Setup edition checks GitHub Releases and downloads available higher versions. Stop trading and settle or resolve open contracts before choosing Restart & Update. The portable edition does not update itself.

How are saved credentials handled?

The Windows app saves your token encrypted using Windows DPAPI through Electron safeStorage. This webpage does not ask for your App ID or token.

Does a trading strategy guarantee profit?

No. Digit frequencies, indicators and candle patterns describe historical data and cannot guarantee future results. Automated trading and loss-recovery strategies can lose money.
